Grounding Context (Codebase Context)

Current State: HomeGym represents each profile's program as a sessions array containing superset groups, target blocks, progression increments (e.g., 2.5kg or 5kg steps), warm-up/cooldown routines, and custom exercise IDs. State is synchronized in real-time to Firebase Realtime Database at gym_users/${profileId}/active_program/current.

Pain Points & Friction:

  • Extraction Gap: Currently, sharing your regime with an AI or physio requires copying a raw developer JSON backup, or manually typing all sessions, sets, and working weights.
  • Ingestion Friction: When an AI or physio suggests changes (e.g. swapping Barbell RDL for Single-Leg DB RDL due to hamstring strain), the user must click through dozens of in-app inputs or risk hand-crafting JSON that could corrupt exercise history.
  • Clinical Load Awareness: Standard workout apps treat changes as binary overwrites, losing track of why a movement was modified, what tissue restriction triggered it, and when to safely progress back.

Leverage Points: app-redesign.js already contains dedicated normalization utilities (normalizeExerciseName), 1RM estimators, and modular session/program structures that can be mapped to an AI exchange protocol.

Topic Axes

  1. Regime Serialization & Prompt Generation: Packaging workouts, equipment, and recent weights into an LLM-ready prompt with clinical guardrails.
  2. Clinical Substitution & Regression Hierarchy: Rules for adjusting external load, ROM, unilateral balance, and 24-hour symptom tracking.
  3. Interactive Ingestion & Safe Diff Review: Visual before/after verification for proposed AI changes without risking stored history.
  4. Regime Versioning & Longitudinal History: Tracking program iterations over time (e.g., Block 1 → Rehab Phase 1 → Return-to-Sport).

1. "Consult AI with Physio Advice" One-Tap Prompt Bundler
Serialization Conf: 94% Cx: Low

Add a dedicated "Export for AI / Physio" action in the Plan tab that generates a pre-formatted, highly structured Markdown prompt ready to paste into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. The prompt bundles your active split, exercise roster, current working weights, available equipment, and explicit clinical instructions (e.g. "My physio advised: [PASTE PHYSIO NOTES HERE]. Regress or substitute affected movements while preserving weekly volume and output in HomeGym JSON format").

Axis: Regime Serialization & Prompt Generation
Basis: direct: In app-redesign.js:7578, the app already exports full JSON backups; transforming this into a human-readable prompt tailored for LLM reasoning eliminates 90% of user copy-paste friction.
Rationale: LLMs excel at strength conditioning and clinical regression when given precise starting context (current weights, sets, equipment). Zero barrier to entry: requires no backend changes or API keys.
Downsides: Requires user to switch apps (HomeGym → ChatGPT → HomeGym).
2. Clinical Substitution Engine with 24-Hour Pain Rules
Substitution Conf: 90% Cx: Medium

Embed clinical substitution hierarchies directly into exercise configurations. Each exercise defines structured regressions along specific clinical levers: Range of Motion (e.g. Barbell Squat → Box Squat), External Load distribution (e.g. Back Squat → Goblet Squat to reduce lumbar shear), or Stability (Bilateral → Supported Unilateral). Incorporates the clinical 24-hour symptom rule: if post-workout soreness or pain persists past 24 hours, the app automatically suggests stepping down one tier.

Axis: Clinical Substitution & Regression Hierarchy
Basis: external: Sports Physiotherapy tissue-capacity rules (Elevate Health, 2026): "Change only one variable at a time (Load → Volume → ROM → Velocity); regress immediately if symptoms persist >24h."
Rationale: Gives you instant, physiologically sound exercise swaps on your phone when feeling joint pain, without waiting for your next physio appointment.
Downsides: Requires curating regression chains for primary compound lifts (RDL, Squat, Bench, Row).
3. Interactive Visual Regime Diff Reviewer & Merger
Ingestion Conf: 96% Cx: Medium

Add a "Paste AI Workout Modification" modal in Settings / Plan. When you paste the response from ChatGPT or Claude, HomeGym parses the changes and presents a visual, color-coded diff: exercises added (green), modified (amber, showing changed sets/reps/tempo), and removed/regressed (red). You can uncheck individual modifications before applying, ensuring AI hallucinations never corrupt your program.

Axis: Interactive Ingestion & Safe Diff Review
Basis: reasoned: In code development, engineers never blindly apply patches without reviewing the diff. Applying the same concept to workout modifications prevents accidental deletion of routines or sudden dangerous weight spikes.
Rationale: Solves the second half of the workflow: getting altered workouts back into the app safely without manually re-typing 15 exercises.
Downsides: Requires client-side schema validation to safely handle malformed LLM outputs.
4. Standing Physio Movement Constraints Layer
Versioning Conf: 92% Cx: Low

Store persistent clinical guidelines under each profile (e.g., "Right knee patellar tendinopathy: limit deep knee flexion under load, substitute knee extension for isometric Spanish squats, 3-sec eccentric tempos"). When active, these constraints appear as reminders during your guided workout and are automatically appended into any future AI prompt export, so the AI never forgets your injury history.

Axis: Regime Versioning & Longitudinal History
Basis: direct: In app-redesign.js:8023, currentProfile already holds persistent fields (goals, resumeNote, equipment). Adding a structured physioConstraints field integrates seamlessly with current sync logic.
Rationale: Prevents context loss over time. As you recover, you can toggle constraints on and off.
Downsides: Users must remember to update or deactivate restrictions once tissue heals.
5. "Return-to-Sport" Rehab Progression Ladders
Substitution Conf: 88% Cx: Medium

Replace static 4-week block weight ladders with clinical multi-stage rehabilitation progressions. For example, progressing from Isometric Hold → Slow Tempo Eccentric → Full Bilateral → Explosive / Sport-Specific. Each stage defines exit criteria (e.g. 3 consecutive sessions with feeling ≥ 4 and pain score 0) before auto-unlocking the next movement tier.

Axis: Clinical Substitution & Regression Hierarchy
Basis: direct: The current codebase already models 4-block ladders (exercise.blocks = [43, 46, 48.5, 51] and exercise.variation). Generalizing this to stage criteria unlocks true clinical rehab tracking.
Rationale: Prevents jumping back into heavy compound lifts too early, reducing re-injury rates.
Downsides: Adds conceptual complexity to the Plan Tab.
6. Post-Session Physio Executive Summary Exporter
Serialization Conf: 95% Cx: Low

Generate a concise, 1-page PDF or formatted shareable text summary for your physio ahead of your next appointment. Summarizes total volume per movement pattern, subjective feeling / RPE ratings, flagged pain events, and readiness scores across the last 2–4 weeks so your physio has objective clinical data rather than relying on memory.

Axis: Regime Serialization & Prompt Generation
Basis: external: Clinical adherence studies show physiotherapists alter exercise prescriptions significantly more accurately when provided objective compliance and symptom logs.
Rationale: Strengthens the feedback loop between you, your physio, and your training tracker.
Downsides: Generates an export artifact that requires email or messaging to send to the physio.
7. Program Versioning & Milestone Changelog
Versioning Conf: 91% Cx: Medium

Store routine adaptations as tagged milestones (e.g. v1.0 Baseline → v1.1 Physio Hamstring Deload → v1.2 Return to Barbell RDL) with commit notes. Allows you to view a timeline of how your program evolved over the year and rollback or restore a previous routine with a single tap.

Axis: Regime Versioning & Longitudinal History
Basis: reasoned: Athletes frequently want to remember "what was that lower body split that felt great back in June before my ankle sprain?". A changelog keeps routine history without polluting active training.
Rationale: Empowers continuous experimentation without fear of permanently losing working routines.
Downsides: Slight storage footprint increase in Realtime Database.

Rejection Summary

Ideas considered during divergent generation that were filtered out during adversarial critique:

# Candidate Idea Reason Rejected
1 Direct in-app Gemini API audio recording of physio sessions Over-engineered & privacy/consent barrier: recording clinical consultations in a gym tracker exceeds scope and incurs ongoing API token costs.
2 Full automated weekly AI re-programming without human approval Safety violation: automated exercise changes without user verification can prescribe contraindicated movements during injury recovery.
3 Real-time computer vision camera tracking for form breakdown Impractical hardware & battery burden for phone-based PWA; does not address the core routine evolution challenge.
4 Crowdsourced physio exercise substitution database Regulatory & clinical liability risk: exercise substitution must be tailored to individual diagnoses, not crowdsourced votes.
5 Wearable EMG sensor integration Hardware dependency: violates low-friction mobile PWA philosophy.
